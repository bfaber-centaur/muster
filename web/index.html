Muster is a small incident-replay harness. Every replay on this page runs in your browser, on an engine checked against the Go engine on every replays, which cover every combination of defenses in every scenario. Scenarios are compressed abstractions, not forensic reconstructions. Source and scenario files: github.com/bfaber-centaur/muster.
